I am a fourth-year PhD student at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai, working in arithmetic geometry. My research focuses on Brauer groups and their generalizations to higher cohomology classes introduced by Kato.

Research

My main area of interest is arithmetic geometry, particularly Brauer groups and higher cohomology groups, their associated period–index problems, and local–global principles.
